The contract as a whole

Summary
VaccineProcurementC is an agreement between pfizer (Manufacturer) and mcdc (Government). regulator (Regulator), fda (FDA), and worldcourier (WorldCourier) take part as third parties without being bound by it.
Fixed when the contract is created
  • approval (Boolean)
  • unitPrice (Number)
  • minQuantity (Number)
  • maxQuantity (Number)
Comes into force only if
vaccineDose.FDAapproval holds
Time is counted in days.
What the parties owe
  • 4 obligations within the contract
  • 2 obligations that survive termination
  • 2 powers
How it can end
  • mcdc may terminate the contract once either mcdc (lawStopWork) or regulator (adminStopWork, regulationStopWork, or judicialStopWork) issues a stop-work order (pStopWork)
  • pfizer may terminate the contract once obligation oRequestVaccineDosage has been fulfilled (pTermination)
Still owed after it ends
  • oPay (mcdc owes pfizer)
  • oWithdrewApproval (pfizer owes mcdc)
Who controls access to information
regulator administers the access-control policy. 9 rules: 4 grants, 5 revocations.
Monitoring
  • temperature (Alert): condition value > -80, 5 readings, and within 10; raised by regulator, controlled by pfizer
  • humidity (Alert): condition value > 70, 3 readings, and within 15; raised by regulator, controlled by pfizer
  • shock (Alert): condition value > 5; raised by regulator, controlled by pfizer
  • lightExposure (Alert): condition value > 0; raised by regulator, controlled by pfizer
  • sealOpen (Alert): condition value > 0; raised by regulator, controlled by pfizer
Normal course (obligations in dependency order)
  1. oAgreedOnRequest: mcdc owes pfizer; created by requested; binding once agreedFromG; requires leadtimeINform.
  2. oDeliver: pfizer owes mcdc; created by requested; binding once confirmed and oAgreedOnRequest is fulfilled; requires notifiedOD, delivered, and outsideRisk.
  3. oAssign: mcdc owes pfizer; created by requested; binding once delivered; requires oDeliver is fulfilled.
  4. oRequestVaccineDosage: mcdc owes pfizer; exists from the start; binding immediately; requires mcdcTerminateAgreement and pfizerTerminateAgreement and oAgreedOnRequest, oDeliver, and oAssign are fulfilled.
  5. oPay (survives termination): mcdc owes pfizer; created by requested; binding once invoiced and delivered and oDeliver is fulfilled; requires paid.
  6. oWithdrewApproval (survives termination): pfizer owes mcdc; exists from the start; binding once withdrewApproval.
Observations
  • No postconditions are specified.
  • No constraints are specified.
